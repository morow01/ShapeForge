Privacy Policy

ShapeForge · Last updated 9 October 2026

ShapeForge is a free 3D design tool that runs entirely in your web browser. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: ShapeForge has no server of its own and collects nothing about you.

Your designs

Designs, folders, tags and settings are stored in your own browser on your own device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, ShapeForge asks Google for these permissions:

  • drive.file – lets ShapeForge create files in your Google Drive and open the files it created. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Your basic profile (name, email address and picture) – used only to show which Google account is connected.

Your designs are saved in a folder called ShapeForge in your Google Drive. The data travels directly between your browser and Google. The developer of ShapeForge never receives, sees or stores your files, your Google sign-in or your profile details.

ShapeForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to save and open your ShapeForge designs, and is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Disconnecting and deleting

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in ShapeForge, or remove its access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files ShapeForge saved stay in your Drive until you delete them. Clearing your browser's site data deletes the copies stored in the browser.

No tracking

ShapeForge uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
